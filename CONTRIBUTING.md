# Contributing to Kaiser Member Service

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Kaiser Member Service application! Your support helps us build a better tool for members managing their healthcare expenses.

Please review the guidelines below to ensure a smooth and collaborative development process.

---

## Code of Conduct
By participating in this project, you agree to maintain a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment. We expect all contributors to adhere to standard harassment-free guidelines, ensuring a welcoming space for everyone regardless of background or experience level.

## How to Contribute
1. **Fork the Repository**: Create a personal copy of the repository on your GitHub account.
2. **Clone Locally**: Clone your fork to your local machine.
3. **Create a Feature Branch**: Keep your changes isolated by creating a branch off `main`:
   ```bash
   git checkout -b feature/your-feature-name
